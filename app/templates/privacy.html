Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What we store

  • Your email address, and when you confirmed it and agreed to the terms.
  • Your topics and the number of papers per topic you asked for.
  • Your send history: when each digest went out.
  • For rate limits, your IP address and email address only as hashed keys, deleted after two days.

We use this only to send you the digest and the emails you ask for (confirmation and manage links). We don't sell it, show ads, or track whether you open or click our emails.

Who processes it

  • Hetzner hosts the server and database.
  • Resend sends our emails, so it sees your email address and the digest.
  • OpenAI writes summaries and ratings. It sees topic phrases and paper abstracts, never your email address.
  • Cloudflare runs the Turnstile check on the signup form, which sees your IP address and browser.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts these pages use, so Google sees your IP address and browser when a page loads.

Where it's stored

On a server in the European Union (Germany or Finland).

Your choices

The manage page linked from every digest lets you edit your topics, pause, unsubscribe, or delete your data. Deleting removes your email address and subscriptions right away; nightly backups that still hold them are deleted within 14 days. To get your manage link again, use "Already subscribed?" on the signup page.

Other things

We don't track you across other sites, so browser Do Not Track signals don't change anything here. The service is not for children under 13. Questions: email kianhekmatnejad@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
