Terms of use

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The service

Papers Weekly is a free weekly email that lists new arXiv papers on topics you choose, with short summaries and an overview. It's offered as is, with no warranty, and we may change or stop it at any time.

Who can use it

You must live in the United States and be 13 or older. Sign up only an email address that's yours.

AI-written text

Summaries, overviews and rankings are written by AI from each paper's abstract. They may be wrong or leave out something important, so read the paper before relying on it. Nothing in a digest is professional advice.

The papers

Papers belong to their authors; we link to them on arXiv and never send their full text. Thank you to arXiv for use of its open access interoperability. Papers Weekly is not affiliated with arXiv, and arXiv does not endorse it.

Leaving

Every digest has an unsubscribe link and a link to your manage page, where you can also delete your data.

Changes

If these terms change, the new version is posted here with a new date.
